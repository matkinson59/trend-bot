Descriptive only. Every series here was already run for bot 2 (trials 12 to 16 and `results/bot2/diagnostics.md`), and the mix's better result was seen before this file was written, so nothing here is a test. Period: Feb 12, 2007 to Nov 20, 2026, counted from the $100,000 start, so version 2 shows 4.9% a year.

## 3. Deflated Sharpe ratio

Counting the 18 logged trials plus the mix itself (19): the mix 0.945, version 2 0.887 (bar 0.95). The best of 19 unskilled trials would be expected to reach a Sharpe ratio of about 0.17 by luck. The mix was picked after seeing it, which this count cannot fully correct for.
